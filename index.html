Our Story

Built on Faith, Grounded in Scripture

4Kingdom Podcast began in Sebring, Florida in 2025, born out of a simple conviction: people are hungry for honest, Christ-centered conversations that don't shy away from the hard questions and don't lead with judgment.

Alexander Roman and Micah had been having these kinds of conversations privately for years. Over coffee, after church, late into evenings, they talked about faith, doubt, Scripture, everyday struggle, and what it actually means to walk with Jesus. Eventually, they decided those conversations needed to be heard by more people.

The name "4Kingdom" is a declaration of purpose. Everything we do is for the Kingdom of God. Every episode, every story, every question we explore points back to Jesus and His Kingdom. It is not about building a platform. It is about building people.

Isaiah 41:10 sits at the center of everything. "Do not fear, for I am with you." That promise is the heartbeat of this ministry. No matter where a listener is in their faith journey, they are welcome here, and they are not alone.
